What affects a catering quote

  • Number of guests. The final count matters, and many caterers ask for a minimum guarantee.
  • Menu size. The number of dishes, live counters and sweets changes ingredient and labour needs.
  • Type of dishes. Dry fruits, paneer-heavy dishes and special sweets cost more than simple dishes.
  • Service style. A buffet, a seated service and live counters need different staffing.
  • Crockery and serveware. Modern crockery, covered serving dishes or eco-friendly serveware may be priced differently.
  • Staff. The number of trained service staff and supervisors needed for your guest count.
  • Venue and distance. Transport and setup depend on where the event is held.
  • Timing. Peak wedding dates and festival days are busy for every caterer.

How to compare two quotes

A lower total is not always a better deal. Before you compare, make sure both quotes cover the same things:

  • Is the price per plate or a package?
  • Which dishes, counters and sweets are included?
  • Is serving staff included, and how many?
  • Are crockery, tables and counters included?
  • Are taxes and transport included?
  • What happens if the guest count goes up or down?

Questions to ask before you pay an advance

  • What is the cancellation or date-change policy?
  • When is the final guest count due?
  • Does the caterer hold an FSSAI licence? Olive's licence number is 10724013000075.
  • Can you taste the main dishes before booking?
