A good wedding catering plan starts months before the first function. Use this checklist to decide what you need, compare caterers fairly and avoid last-minute surprises when you are planning a wedding in Gandhidham or Kutch.

Before you contact caterers

  • List every function that needs food: engagement, mehndi, sangeet, wedding, reception, farewell.
  • Estimate guest numbers for each function, and note which guests are Jain.
  • Fix your dates and venues, and check whether the venue has its own catering rules.
  • Decide a rough budget range for catering.

When you shortlist caterers

  • Check the FSSAI licence number and ask about the kitchen's hygiene practices.
  • Confirm the caterer can handle your menu style, such as pure veg and Jain.
  • Ask about live counters, buffet setups and serving staff.
  • Ask to see photos or past events, and for references.
  • Ask whether a tasting is possible.

Before you book

  • Get a written quote listing dishes, counters, staff, crockery and taxes.
  • Read the payment schedule and the cancellation policy.
  • Confirm the date for the final guest count and how changes are handled.
  • Confirm who handles power, water and space at the venue.

One to two weeks before

  • Share the final guest count for each function.
  • Finalise the menu, including Jain dishes and any allergies guests have told you about.
  • Confirm serving times and the timing of each live counter.
  • Agree the buffet layout with the venue and decorator.

On the day

  • Name one family member as the point of contact for the caterer.
  • Make sure Jain dishes are labelled and kept separate.
  • Ask staff to serve elders and children first if needed.
  • Check food is covered and served at the right temperature.
